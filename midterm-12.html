Timing and units: K is a real-capital stock; I is capital investment over the stated period. Capital accumulation is K_next = (1 − δ)K + I. Investment today adds future productive capital; the course usually holds current K and current Y* fixed in the current-I comparison. MPK is a derivative, not an exact finite one-unit gain. Interest and depreciation periods must match. With output price P and capital price p(k), compare P·MPK with p(k)(r + δ). Only with the equal-price assumption does the condition simplify to MPK = r + δ.
