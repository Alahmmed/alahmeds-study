Model and notation: Equal periods, zero real interest, log utility with equal time weights, unrestricted saving and borrowing, and no desired terminal debt or bequest imply equal consumption over the remaining periods. Income y and consumption c are real-dollar amounts during a period. Signed net assets a are measured at the period’s start. At zero interest, a_next = a_now + y_now − c_now. Positive a is saving; negative a is debt. Use disposable income after taxes and transfers. Nonzero interest, different preferences, uncertainty or a binding borrowing constraint require a different calculation.
